# 🐜 MICRO EMPIRE: ANT WARS - Roadmap

## 🟢 Phase 1: Core Foundation (Current Status)
**Status: COMPLETED**
- **Core Engine Complete**: 2D grid-based underground world, tile-based terrain.
- **Digging System Solved**: Player creates tunnel commands, tiles have health, structured formation.
- **Task System Working**: Player generated dig tasks, sequential execution.
- **Colony Structure Stable**: Role-based worker system (Digger/Explorer merging).
- **Core Game Loop**: Player plans → ants execute → tunnels form → space expands.

---

## 🟡 Phase 2: "Living Colony" (Up Next)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> This phase focuses on transitioning the prototype from a static tool into a living, breathing ecosystem with survival pressures and intelligence.

### 1. Multi-Ant Digging System
- Multiple ants work on the same tunnel simultaneously.
- Digging speed scales with the size of the swarm assigned.
- The colony begins to feel like a true collective organism.

### 2. Food & Economy Loop
- **World Spawns**: Food sources generate within the grid.
- **Collection**: Ants gather and transport food back to the Queen.
- **Economy Backbone**: Food becomes the primary currency for survival and population scaling.

### 3. Job Intelligence System
- Replace random/static assignments with dynamic logic.
- **Self-Balancing Workforce**: 
  - Food shortage → Allocate more food collectors.
  - Expansion demand → Allocate more diggers.
  - Danger detected → Deploy scouts/soldiers.

### 4. True Exploration & Fog System
- Unexplored areas of the map remain hidden in fog.
- Ants dynamically reveal the map as they push the colony frontier.
- Vision-driven gameplay makes exploration meaningful.

---

## 🔵 Phase 3: "Emergent Colony"
> [!NOTE]
> This phase introduces advanced colony capabilities, structural progression, and the first signs of hostility.

### 5. Builder System
- Ants gain the ability to reinforce tunnels.
- Create specialized chambers (e.g., storage, nurseries).
- Repair damaged structures.

### 6. Nursery & Population Growth
- Queen produces new ants over time.
- Population scaling is directly tied to the food economy.
- The colony evolves and expands its workforce dynamically.

### 7. Threat System
- **Hazards**: Spiders invading the tunnels, cave-ins collapsing paths.
- **Environmental**: Flooding spreading through open tunnel networks.

---

## 🔴 Phase 4: "Living World Simulation"
> [!WARNING]
> The final phase introduces macro-level challenges, forcing the player to adapt their strategy against external forces.

### 8. Dynamic World Pressure
- Environmental changes and resource scarcity zones.
- Highly dangerous regions requiring specialized ant teams to traverse.

### 9. Rival Colonies (Late Game)
- Competing ant colonies controlled by AI.
- Territorial expansion and underground resource wars.

### 10. Evolution System
- Genetic mutation choices and adaptation paths.
- Colony specialization (e.g., combat-focused vs. efficient diggers).

---

### 🧠 FINAL GAME VISION
At full scale, the game becomes:
> **A real-time underground colony simulation where a living swarm builds, expands, survives, and evolves in a hostile procedural world shaped by both player strategy and autonomous ant intelligence.**
